28 CHURCH OF THE HOLY SEPULCHRE.


Jerusalem ; it is accursed alike in Christian, Jew
and Gentile. 
The annexed ground-plan of the Church of the Holy Sepulchre pretends to no accuracy, either of proportion or detail, being drawn from recollection, and is merely given to afford a general idea of its form, and of the ceremonies performed therein


1. The Holy Sepulchre itself, situated beneath a dome supported by a circular row of marble pillars. 
2. A little chapel attached to it, belonging to Coptic and Abyssinian Christians. 
3. A large slab of marble, marking the spot where the body of our Saviour is said to have been embalmed.
4. Divan, where the Turkish toll-gatherers sit.
5. Chapel of the Crucifixion, up a flight of steep stairs, beneath which are places of refreshment
6, 7, 10. Cloisters. 
8. Body of the Church, appropriated to the Greeks.
9. Chapel of St. Helena, where the cross is said to have been discovered by her.
11. Chapel belonging to Roman Catholics
