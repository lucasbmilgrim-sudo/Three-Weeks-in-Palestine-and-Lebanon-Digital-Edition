At no great distance from the Holy Gate is that of St. Stephen, through which the proto-martyr was led out to be stoned; close to which is a large and deep cistern, now very scantily supplied with water, said to be that built by King Hezekiah; parallel with which, but within the wall, is the Pool of Bethesda. Hence we descended to the brook Kedron, a mere mountain-torrent, whose bed was perfectly dry at the time we crossed the arch which spanned it.
