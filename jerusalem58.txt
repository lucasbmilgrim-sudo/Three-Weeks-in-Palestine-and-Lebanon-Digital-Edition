Jowett, in his Researches, quoting the remark of the disciples, " Master, see what manner of stones and what buildings are here!" hazards the supposi- tion that they might have belonged to the Jewish Temple; but, as our Saviour doomed it to utter destruction, and history relates that "the plough- share passed over the beauty of that house," I think the supposition scarcely probable. Besides, these blocks, though of limestone, exhibit no traces of the action of fire, which must have been the case had they been submitted to that devouring flame which left not one stone upon another, and, as Josephus informs us, made the very mountain, upon which the Temple stood, appear as if it had been one body of fire.
